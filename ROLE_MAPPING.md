## How To Read The Portfolio

The strongest signal is not advanced modelling. The strongest signal is that the work follows a repeatable analyst loop:

1. Define the raw event or case clearly.
2. Apply simple, explainable rules.
3. Put high-risk cases into a review queue.
4. Write a reviewer note that explains the evidence.
5. Connect separate risk scenarios into one operational workload view.
6. Summarise KRIs for manager review.
7. Name the boundary where a human reviewer or authorised specialist must decide.

## Why This Is Useful For Entry-Level Risk Operations

Many junior risk, fraud, compliance operations and AI-workflow roles do not need someone to invent a black-box model on day one. They need someone who can:

- read a policy or procedure carefully
- classify cases consistently
- keep notes clean
- escalate high-risk or unclear matters
- use SQL or Python to summarise a queue
- explain AI output without pretending it is a final decision

That is the evidence this repo is designed to show.
